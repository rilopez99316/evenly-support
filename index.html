FAQ

Good questions.

Do my friends need the app?
To see balances and add expenses themselves, yes — invite them to a group with its invite code and they can join in a few taps. You can also split with friends who aren’t on Evenly yet; they just won’t see the balances.
Can I split something unevenly?
Yes. Split equally, by exact amounts or by percentage. Evenly shows what’s left to assign and only saves once everything adds up.
Which currencies are supported?
24 currencies, including USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, MXN, COP and INR. Set a default for yourself and a different one for any group or expense.
Does Evenly handle recurring bills?
Rent, subscriptions and utilities can repeat weekly, monthly or yearly, and Evenly keeps the next one in view so nobody’s surprised.
How do we actually pay each other back?
Evenly records the payment and updates everyone’s balance. Use whichever payment app you already like for the transfer itself.
Can I export my data?
You can export any group’s expenses as a CSV, or all of your data from your profile, whenever you like.
